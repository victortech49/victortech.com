Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay in installments?
Yes! We offer flexible payment plans for most projects. Speak to our team to arrange a payment schedule that works for you.
Can prices be negotiated?
Absolutely. Every project is unique, and we're happy to discuss custom pricing based on your specific requirements and budget.
Do you redesign existing websites?
Yes! We can redesign or refresh your existing website to give it a modern, professional look while keeping your content.
How long does development take?
Timelines vary by project scope. A simple website takes 3-5 days, while complex projects like e-commerce or portals take 10-20 days.
Do you offer free revisions?
Yes! We offer unlimited revisions until you're 100% satisfied with the final result. Your satisfaction is our priority.
What happens after payment?
Once payment is received, we begin work immediately. You'll receive regular updates and have direct communication with your project manager.
